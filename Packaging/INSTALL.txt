VitalsLoom — unsigned preview
=============================

System requirements
-------------------
macOS 14 or newer on Apple Silicon or Intel.

Install
-------
1. Drag VitalsLoom.app to the Applications shortcut in this window.
2. Open VitalsLoom from Applications once. macOS will block the first launch
   because this preview is not signed with an Apple Developer ID or notarized.
3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
4. Scroll to Security and click Open Anyway for VitalsLoom.
5. Authenticate and click Open in the confirmation dialog.

This creates an exception for VitalsLoom only. Do not disable Gatekeeper.
If macOS says the app will damage your computer or that the download is
corrupted, do not override the warning; delete it and report the problem.

The app starts with simulated data. Live device access requires a separate
authorized service configuration. No third-party credentials or secrets are
included in this disk image.
