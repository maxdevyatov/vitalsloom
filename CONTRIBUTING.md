# Contributing

Contributions should preserve VitalsLoom's independent identity, privacy, and
safety limitations.

1. Build with `swift build` and test changes in demo mode first.
2. Do not commit credentials, tokens, proprietary service configuration,
   device serials, databases, health readings, or user logs.
3. Do not add third-party trademarks to the app name, icon, bundle identifier,
   or primary branding, or imply sponsorship or medical certification.
4. Keep the first-run safety acknowledgment and the README safety warning
   accurate when changing monitoring or alarm behavior.
5. Add attribution for incorporated third-party code and confirm its license
   is compatible with MIT before submitting it.

By submitting a contribution, you agree that it may be distributed under the
project's MIT License and confirm that you have the right to provide it.
